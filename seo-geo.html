Why both

Search did not replace Google. It grew a second door.

Google is still where commercial intent lives: "near me", comparison queries, service pages, local packs. If you skip classic SEO, you are arguing with ten years of how people buy.

GEO is the second door. Google's AI Overviews sit on top of many informational queries. ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity pull from pages they can parse: clear entities, dated facts, author names, and copy that answers the question in the first screen. If your brand facts are messy across the web, the model picks whoever looks cleaner.

What we actually optimize for

Classic SEO

The ten blue links, Maps, and the snippet

Indexation, titles, internal links, speed, and pages that match search intent - not a blog post stuffed with a keyword.

  • Crawl, canonicals, robots, and Search Console coverage
  • Intent map: service pages vs guides vs local
  • Titles, H1s, and internal links on money pages first
GEO

The answer box that never clicks through

Models summarize. They prefer pages with a named organization, a person, a date, and a direct answer - then a source they can keep.

  • JSON-LD for Organization, Service, FAQ, and Article
  • Answer-first sections, not fluff intros
  • Same NAP, same offers, same claims across the web
Scope

What sits in the monthly work

This is the stack. We do not park schema, content, or reporting in a later "phase two" after you have already paid.

01

Technical pass you can ticket

We crawl the live site and write a punch list: blocked URLs, duplicate titles, thin service pages, broken internals, Core Web Vitals that are actually hurting templates. You get the list in language a developer can ship.

02

Keyword set tied to real pages

We do not dump 400 keywords in a sheet and call it strategy. You approve a set. Each term maps to a URL that exists or a URL we agree to build. If the page cannot win, we say so.

03

On-page on the pages that take money

Titles, headings, first 100 words, FAQ blocks, and internal links on service and location pages. Blog comes after those pages are not embarrassing.

04

Entity and schema so machines stop guessing

Organization, Person, Service, and FAQ markup. Author lines on articles. One canonical description of who you are, what you sell, and where you operate - the same wording you would say on a call.

05

Mentions, not a backlink farm

Useful citations, profiles, and pages that already talk about your category. We will not buy expired domains or run a private blog network. If a mention is junk, it stays off the plan.

06

A report a founder can read in ten minutes

Search Console coverage, the agreed keyword set, pages that moved, AI Overview / assistant notes where we can observe them, and the next 30 days of work. Screenshots, not a 40-page PDF nobody opens.
