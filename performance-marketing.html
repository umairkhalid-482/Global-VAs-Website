The difference

A media budget is not a strategy. A tested system is.

Every platform's dashboard is built to make its own numbers look favorable. Meta counts a "result" differently than Google counts a conversion. Left unchecked, that gap quietly inflates a budget that appears to be working while actual sales remain flat.

We do not run ads to hit a reach number. Every campaign is built around one metric your business already tracks - cost per lead, cost per sale, or return on ad spend - and every creative, audience, and platform choice is judged against it.

Meta, Google, TikTok, and every other channel in your mix are not managed as disconnected accounts telling separate stories. One conversion event, one attribution window, and a single, unified read on what genuinely worked.

No inflated reach numbers You will not see a slide full of impressions and reach. Reporting starts from your pixel and conversion data, not a platform's default dashboard.
A learning phase before we scale New accounts or new offers require time to gather enough data. We set that expectation upfront instead of promising day-one results we cannot substantiate.
Your budget only funds what works An underperforming ad set is paused, not defended in a monthly call. What is proven gets more budget - what is not, does not.
Two jobs

Awareness spend and conversion spend are not the same job

Different goal, different metric, different creative. Treating them as one budget is how a campaign looks busy and books nothing.

Top of funnel

Reaching people who are not yet aware of your brand

Broad reach on Meta, TikTok, Google, and YouTube, judged on cost per reach and engagement - not sales, because that is not the function this spend is intended to perform.

  • Broad and lookalike audience testing
  • 4-6 creative concepts tested before scaling any one
  • Judged on CPM, hook rate, and watch time - not conversions
Bottom of funnel

Closing the people who already raised a hand

Retargeting and search-intent campaigns on Meta and Google, judged on cost per acquisition and return on ad spend - where the actual sale gets counted.

  • Retargeting warm traffic and cart abandoners
  • Search campaigns on high-intent, buyer-ready keywords
  • Judged on CPA and ROAS against your agreed target
Scope

What sits in the monthly work

This is the stack. We do not park creative testing or reporting in a later "phase two" after payment has already been made.

01

An account review you can act on

We review pixel setup, past campaign data, and account structure, and return a list of what is broken, where spend is being wasted, and what is worth retaining - in an order you can action.

02

Audience and offer mapping

We map your actual buyers against each platform's targeting tools, and agree which offer runs at which funnel stage before a single dollar is spent.

03

Creative built to be tested, not guessed

Four to six ad concepts per campaign at launch, each isolated so we can identify precisely which hook, image, or angle is performing - not a single flagship creative running on hope.

04

Pixel and conversion tracking implemented correctly

Meta Pixel, Conversions API, and Google Tag Manager configured and verified against genuine form submissions or purchases - not merely installed and left unattended.

05

Budget moved weekly, not monthly

Underperforming ad sets are paused inside the week. Budget shifts toward what is converting, instead of waiting for a monthly call to notice.

06

A report a founder can read in ten minutes

Spend, CPA, ROAS, and what changed this week, communicated in plain language against the metric you track - not a screenshot of a platform's dashboard.

First quarter

What the first 90 days look like

If pixel access or creative approvals are late, month one slips. We need ad account access, a fulfillment plan, and someone who can approve creative.

Month 1

Set up and learn

  • Access, pixel review, and tracking fixes
  • Audience and offer map for your sign-off
  • First campaigns launch in testing mode
  • No scaling decisions yet - this month is data
Month 2

Find what works

  • Discontinue underperforming ad sets and angles
  • Scale the combinations that are converting
  • Retargeting and search campaigns go live
  • First real CPA / ROAS numbers against target
Month 3

Scale what is proven

  • Budget shifts toward the winning campaigns
  • New creative batch based on what performed
  • First honest quarter review against target
  • Plan for quarter two, based on what moved
Fit

Who this is for and who should wait

This works if you

  • Have a clear offer and the ability to fulfil new demand
  • Can approve creative and copy within a few days
  • Have, or will set up, working pixel and conversion tracking
  • Can fund a testing phase before expecting scale

Skip this if you

  • Need a sale this week with zero prior data
  • Cannot approve creative without a multi-week internal process
  • Have no way to fulfil a sudden spike in orders - we would recommend addressing operations first
  • Want a specific ROAS guaranteed before we have even reviewed your account
